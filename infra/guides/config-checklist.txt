## 📂 STEP 1: CREATE 2 GOOGLE DRIVE FOLDERS
Go to Google Drive and create two folders:
1. Folder A (name it: "Channel A — Ready to Publish")
2. Folder B (name it: "Channel B — Ready to Publish")

Double-click each folder and copy the ID from the browser address bar:
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/<<<THIS_IS_THE_ID>>>

## 📸 CHANNEL A (Your Recorded Videos) — WHAT TO CONFIG

### Workflow 1: videoEditingLocal.json (Local Free Editor)
Open this workflow in n8n and set:
1. **"Grab Video Files (Local)" node**:
   - Airtable Credential: Select your Airtable Token
   - Base & Table: Choose your Video clips base/table
2. **"Grab Music File (Local)" node**:
   - Base & Table: Choose your Music base/table
   - ID: Select your background audio record ID
3. **"Upload to Publish Folder" node**:
   - Google Drive Credential: Select `Google Drive — Account A`
   - Folder ID: `FOLDER_A_ID`

### Workflow 3: workflow-personal.json (Publish Channel A)
Open this workflow in n8n and set:
1. **"Google Drive Trigger (Channel A)" node**:
   - Google Drive Credential: Select `Google Drive — Account A`
   - Folder to Watch: `FOLDER_A_ID`
2. **"Telegram Verification (A)" node**:
   - Chat ID: `YOUR_TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`
3. **"Post to Instagram (Free)" node**:
   - Instagram Credential: Select `Instagram Business — Account A`
4. **"YouTube Upload (A)" and "YouTube Thumbnail (A)" nodes**:
   - YouTube Credential: Select `YouTube OAuth2 — Account A`
